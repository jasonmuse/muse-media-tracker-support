Muse Media Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Overview

Muse Media Tracker is designed as a personal media library. The app stores your library, categories, statuses, profiles, notes, ratings, and related preferences locally on your device. Optional account, cloud-library, and connected-app features are described below.

Information You Add

You may add media titles, custom items, categories, profiles, notes, ratings, statuses, and backup files. This information is used to provide the app's library, organization, discovery, and backup features.

Backups

If you export a backup, the backup file is created for you to store or share outside the app. If you import a backup, the app uses the file you select to restore library data on your device.

Optional Accounts and Cloud Library

If you create a Muse account, Muse uses your email address and account identifier to sign you in. Signing in with Google or Apple also shares the identity information you authorize, such as your name and email address, with Muse through Supabase Authentication. Muse does not receive your Google or Apple password. Apple may provide a private relay email address if you choose Hide My Email.

Signing in does not automatically copy your device library to your account. If you choose to use cloud-library features, the profiles, media entries, notes, ratings, covers, and other library data you copy or add there are stored using Supabase and processed by Muse's Cloudflare-hosted service. Cloud changes can retain previous versions to support activity history and undo. Purchase validation services may associate your Muse account with its purchase entitlement.

Connected Apps and AI Services

If you connect another app or AI service, it can access the Muse profiles and read or edit permissions you authorize. You can revoke that access in Muse. Information shared with a connected service is also subject to that service's privacy policy.

Metadata and Online Features

When you search for media, view details, browse Explore, or open trailers, screenshots, wiki links, cast, crew, or other online information, the app may request data from third-party metadata and content services. Those services may receive technical information such as your device's IP address and the search or content request needed to return results.

Ads and Purchases

Muse Media Tracker may show ads and offers an optional non-consumable ad-free upgrade. Advertising, purchase, and restore-purchase features may be processed by third-party services such as Apple, ad networks, or purchase validation providers. Those services process data according to their own privacy policies and platform rules.

Children's Privacy

Muse Media Tracker is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the support process, open a support request so it can be addressed.

Data Deletion

You can remove library data from the app by deleting items, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. Backup files exported outside the app must be deleted from the locations where you saved or shared them.

Signing out or uninstalling does not delete your cloud account or its stored library and activity history. For help deleting cloud account data, open a support request. Do not post passwords, sign-in codes, tokens, or private library information in a public GitHub issue.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, app support, purchase issues, bug reports, feedback, or feature requests, open a support request on GitHub.
